# 🧠 MINDLOCK: Digital Discipline System - User Guide

MindLock is more than just a reminder app; it's a **Digital Discipline System** designed to help you reclaim your focus and build consistent habits. This guide covers all the features and how they work together to improve your productivity.

---

## 1. 🏠 The Dashboard (Home)
The heart of the app where your day is summarized.
- **Greeting & Greeting Controller**: Personalized greetings based on the time of day.
- **Discipline Score**: A live percentage calculated based on your task completion and focus consistency.
- **Current Streak**: Tracks how many consecutive days you've completed your core tasks. Don't break the chain!
- **Today's Tasks**: Quick access to your most immediate reminders.
- **AI Smart Suggestions**: Real-time advice generated to help you stay on track (e.g., suggesting a break or a focus session).

---

## 2. 🔔 Smart Reminders
MINDLOCK uses a "hard" notification system to ensure you never miss what's important.
- **Priority Levels**:
    - **Low**: Simple notification.
    - **Medium**: Regular reminders.
    - **High**: Persistent alerts.
    - **Emergency**: Critical alerts that demand immediate attention.
- **Auto-Repeat**: Reminders can be set to repeat automatically if not marked as "Done," ensuring you don't just swipe them away.
- **Navigation**: Tabs for **Today**, **Upcoming**, and **Completed** tasks to keep your schedule organized.

---

## 3. 🚀 Mission Mode (Focus System)
When you need to get work done, you "Initialize a Mission." This triggers the app's strict focus mode.

### Intensity Levels:
1. **Light Focus**: A standard timer with gentle reminders to stay on task.
2. **Medium Focus**: The app actively monitors your phone and blocks distracting apps like Instagram, YouTube, and Games.
3. **Hardcore Focus**: **Total Lockdown.** The app tracks "escape attempts." If you leave the focus screen, your discipline score takes a heavy hit.

### 👥 Co-Focus Buddy (Multiplayer Focus)
- Create or join a 4-digit room.
- **Shared Punishment**: If any one person in the room breaks their focus or fails the mission, **everyone fails.** This uses social accountability to keep you disciplined.

---

## 4. 📊 Analytics & Progress
Track your journey toward peak discipline.
- **Visual Charts**: See your discipline score trends over weeks and months.
- **Task completion rates**: Breakdown of how many missions and reminders you've finished.
- **Streak History**: Celebrate your longest periods of consistency.

---

## 5. ⚙️ Settings & Core Systems
- **Cloud Backup**: Sync your stats and reminders to the cloud (requires Google login).
- **Appearance**: Dark/Light/System theme modes with a premium glassmorphism design.
- **Permissions (Crucial for Functionality)**:
    - **Display Over Other Apps**: Needed for full-screen reminder overlays.
    - **Accessibility Service**: This is the "brain" that monitors which apps you are using to block distractions during missions.
    - **Battery Optimization**: Ensures the app stays alive in the background for reliable alarms.

---

## 🛠️ How to use MINDLOCK (Working Flow)

### Scenario A: Daily Task Management
1. Open the app and tap the **+** button.
2. Create a reminder for a task (e.g., "Gym at 6:00 PM").
3. Set priority to **High** if it's non-negotiable.
4. When the alarm goes off, it will stay on your screen until you tap **Done**.

### Scenario B: Deep Work Study Session
1. Tap **Mission Mode**.
2. Set a title (e.g., "Exam Prep") and duration (60 mins).
3. Select **Hardcore Intensity** to block all distractions.
4. (Optional) Turn on **Co-Focus** and share the room code with a friend.
5. Tap **Initialize Mission**. Your phone is now a tool, not a distraction.
